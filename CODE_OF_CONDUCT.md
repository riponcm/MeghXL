# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. This is a small project about moving files around a network;
nothing here is worth being unpleasant over.

## Expected behaviour

- Assume good faith. Most disagreements are misunderstandings.
- Critique code, not people.
- Accept that maintainers may decline a contribution. A "no" to a patch is not a
  judgement of the person who wrote it.
- Remember that many contributors are not writing in their first language.

## Unacceptable behaviour

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing someone's private
information, and sustained disruption of discussions.

## Reporting

Report anything that crosses the line to **riponce.buet [at] gmail [dot] com**.
Reports are handled privately. Maintainers may edit, remove or reject comments,
commits, issues and PRs that don't follow this document, and may ban a contributor
for behaviour they judge to be harmful.

## Scope

This applies in the repository — issues, pull requests, discussions — and anywhere
someone is representing the project publicly.
